In this case, the terms $p \lor q \implies r \lor s$ and $p \lor q \implies (r \lor s)$ are identical. Recall that the convention for implication is such:
$$
p \implies q \implies r \to p \implies (q \implies r)
$$
Where in this case $p = p \lor q$ and $q = r \lor s$.
