Consider the sets $A_{1}, \dots, A_{2}$. 
Define using the summation of unions similar to the summation symbol $\sum$ as below:
$$
\forall e. \left( e \in \bigcup_{i=1}^{n} A_{i} \iff e \in A_{1} \vee \dots \vee e \in A_{n} \right)
$$
The same for intersections
$$
\forall e. \left( e \in \bigcap_{i=1}^{n} A_{i \iff } e \in A_{1} \wedge \dots \wedge e \in A_{n}\right)
$$
