Self Implication:
$$
\forall a . a \implies a 
$$
modus Ponens:
$$
\forall a  . \ a \implies  (a \implies b) \implies b
$$
implication by composition:
$$
\forall a \ b \ c . (a \implies b) \implies (b \implies c) \implies (a \implies c)
$$
false Introduction
$$
\forall a . \bar{a} \implies
$$

Classical logic is all about  proving the truthness of the statement whereas intuitionistic logic would be building the constructive cases
