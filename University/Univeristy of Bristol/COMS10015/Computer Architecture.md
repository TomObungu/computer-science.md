# Part 2: Unit Organisation 

There are three themes throughout the lectures on a higher level. This involves taking concepts within mathematics and physics and turning them into high level abstract concepts of physics. This is a similar approach taken to a course like electronic engineering.

The three themes involve:
	1. From mathematics and physics to digital logic
	2. From digital logic to processors
	3. From computer processors to software applications

At a lower level, the unit involves the following activities:
		Lecture slot -> synchronous i.e timetabled
					-> in-person
		Lab slot -> in-person

The summative assessments are weighted. Meaning they will contribute to the passing the course. You will need to get a percentage point of 40% to pass the course.

Summative assessment. Each summative assessment is out of 20 credit points (CP). 
	1. Summative coursework assignment -> TB1, week 10. This coursework is worth 30% weight. Which is 6 CP.
	2.  Summative exam -> TB1, week 10. This is worth 70% weight. Which is 14 CP.

Thus, you will need to get 8 CP overall at the end of the year on the summative assessment. 

The formative assessments are worth 0CP and there help within the course.
