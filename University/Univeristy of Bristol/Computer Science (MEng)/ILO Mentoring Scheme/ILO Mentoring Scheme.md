You will meet your tutor within the first year. The mentoring scheme is mandatory and will begin in the second year. You are expected to build and maintain an experience with your mentors. 

There are about 280 mentors and you are not allowed to change your mentor

# What mentoring is/isn't
A mentor is someone who teaches or gives help and advice to less a experienced. A process for sharing knowledge. It is an informal session led by you. 

However mentoring ins't academic support, pastoral care or the mentors being experts in the field.

# Who are the mentors
The mentors have worked in a related industry with at least 18 months experience but at any stage in their career. They are all volunteers from small, medium and large companies.
