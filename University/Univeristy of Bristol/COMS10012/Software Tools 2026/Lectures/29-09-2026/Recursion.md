# Triangle Numbers
$$
t:N \to N
t(n) = \begin{cases}
\begin{gather*}
t = 1 \qquad \text{If } n = 1 \\ \\ \\
n + t(n-1) \qquad \text{otherwise}
\end{gather*}
\end{cases}
$$
# Proof
$$
\begin{gather*}
\text{Claim:} \\ \\
t(n) = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} \\ \\
\text{Consider the case: } n = 1 \\ \\
LHS = 1
\end{gather*}
$$
