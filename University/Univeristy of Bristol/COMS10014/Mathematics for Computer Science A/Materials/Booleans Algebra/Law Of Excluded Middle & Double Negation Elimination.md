## The Law Of Excluded Middle
The Law Of Excluded Middle states that if we have, for some propositional statement $\phi$, the scenario of $\phi \lor ¬\phi$. Then the propositional statement $\phi \land ¬\phi$ is unsatisfiable.

These statements are correct precisely because, for any given assignment, a propositional formula may only evaluate to true or false. If we consider  a disjunction a proposition or its negation, then clearly one must be true. Thus it is impossible for of them to be true at the same otherwise there would be a contradiction. That is why, it would not be possible to have $\phi \land ¬\phi$.
