# Propositions 
A proposition is a statement that can be true or false. That is a Boolean valued statement.  Propositions must be evaluated correctly according to the context. For example, "it is raining" depends on the current weather and "$x<y$" depends on the value of $x$ and $y$.

## Propositional Formulas
A propositional formula also refereed to simply a formula or proposition, is a term built from Boolean operators, e.g. conjunction and disjunctions
