For this statement $q\implies r$. The statement would translate to "if it is snowing, then it is raining". The negation of this statement would translate to "It is not the case that, if it is snowing then it is raining". 

A more natural way of saying this is, "if it is not snowing and it is not freezing". 

This is because $q\implies r$ can be expressed as $¬q \lor r$. The negation of this is $q \land ¬r$.
