An ad hoc problem is one in which the algorithm do not fall into standard categories with well studied solutions. Each ad hoc problem is different. No specific or general techniques exist to solve them.

This makes them 'fun' as the novelty of the question presents a new challenge. The solutions might require a novel data structure or an unusual set of loops or conditionals. Sometimes they require special combinations that are rare or at least rarely encountered. 

These types of problems require careful reading and usually yield to an attack that revolves around carefully sequencing the instructions given in the problem. 

These types of problems can still require reasonablt optimizations and at least a degree of analysis that enables one to avoid nested loops.

Always be ready for an ad hoc problem if you cannot classify a problem as one of the other standard types.
